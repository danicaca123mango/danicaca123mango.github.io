About

The Ashland Tutoring Club provides free private tutoring lessons to students from grades K-8, run by local high schoolers!

  • We offer homework help and additional guidance through worksheets and activities
  • School supplies will be provided
  • Lessons are only offered on the weekends
  • Lessons are held at Noble Coffee Roasting (281 4th St, Ashland, OR 97520)
  • Lessons are 30 minutes long
